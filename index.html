Before we celebrate

A few
little details.

Everything else will be shared with the formal invitation.

What should I wear?

Black formal attire. We kindly ask guests to wear a black dress or black suit.

What time should I arrive?

We are still finalizing the ceremony time and the evening’s schedule. Please refer to the formal invitation when it arrives. The calendar download saves the date as an all-day reminder, not a confirmed ceremony time.

How do I RSVP?

Online RSVP access will be shared with the formal invitation. No response is being collected through this site yet.

Is parking available at the venue?

The William Aiken House does not offer on-site parking. Plan for a rideshare or nearby public parking. You’ll find directions and a parking search in the Arrive section.

Is the venue accessible?

The venue states that the house and grounds are accessible, with elevator access to the second floor and accessible restrooms. For a specific accommodation, please contact the venue directly through its official website.

Where are you registered?

Your presence means the most to us. Registry details will be shared with the formal invitation.
